Cabingo 3D prototype

Three.js version 0.180.0, fetched from the official npm package:
https://registry.npmjs.org/three/-/three-0.180.0.tgz
Upstream: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js
License: MIT. The unmodified license is in vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt.
Locally vendored files: three.module.min.js and three.core.min.js.
No CDN or remote runtime asset requests are used. Music is served locally with the game.

The small walking/proximity-task format was inspired by discussion of
https://github.com/andyhuo520/grandma-gta . No source code, models, sounds,
characters, or other assets were copied from that repository.
All cabin geometry, characters, scenario logic and UI are original for this prototype.

Cabingo v0.3: passenger motion, synthesized Web Audio effects and inline SVG task icons are original procedural work. No external audio files, character models or textures were added.
